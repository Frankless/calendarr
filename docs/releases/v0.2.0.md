## What's new

- New look: warm charcoal (cream in light mode) with a single ember accent, following the device's light
  or dark setting.
- "Surprends-moi" (Surprise me) is now a swipeable deck, in three piles: for me, for the two of us, for
  the family. Right schedules the title in the next suitable slot, left never suggests it again, up marks
  it as watched (in Jellyfin too for a movie), down brings it back in two weeks. Buttons and arrow keys
  do the same, and you can undo the last choice.
- Match for two: in "Pour nous deux", each parent swipes on their own account. When you both say yes,
  the movie is scheduled and listed under "Vos matchs".
- The weekly schedule learns from these choices: a rejected title doesn't come back, a liked one is
  favored.
- One Jellyfin user per audience (parents, kids, family), each with its own watch history and series
  progress.
- "On l'a vu" / "Déjà vu" on cards marks movies or episodes as watched in Jellyfin for the right user.
- "Autre idée" opens a list of suggestions for the slot; marking a movie as watched opens the same list.
- The week opens on today with past days folded; each card shows only "Regarder", the rest is in "…".
- "À venir" starts today, with "Prêt à regarder" or "Sort le …".
