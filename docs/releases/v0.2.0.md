## What's new

- New look: warm charcoal (cream in light mode) with a single ember accent, following the device's light
  or dark setting.
- "Surprends-moi" (Surprise me) is now a swipeable deck, in three piles: for me, for the two of us, for
  the family. Right schedules the title in the next suitable slot, left never suggests it again, up marks
  it as watched (in Jellyfin too for a movie), down brings it back in two weeks. Buttons and arrow keys
  do the same, and you can undo the last choice.
- Match for two: in "Pour nous deux", each parent swipes on their own account. When you both say yes,
  the movie is scheduled and listed under "Vos matchs".
- The weekly schedule learns from these choices: a rejected title doesn't come back, a liked one is
  favored.
- One Jellyfin user per audience (parents, kids, family), each with its own watch history and series
  progress.
- "On l'a vu" / "Déjà vu" on cards marks movies or episodes as watched in Jellyfin for the right user.
- "Autre idée" opens a list of suggestions for the slot; marking a movie as watched opens the same list.
- The week opens on today with past days folded; each card shows only "Regarder", the rest is in "…".
- "À venir" starts today, with "Prêt à regarder" or "Sort le …".

### Nothing is scheduled until the family chooses it

- What Calendarr proposes is now an "Idée" (idea). "On le garde" or "On regarde ça" confirms it, and only
  confirmed programs go to the iCloud calendar and the Jellyfin collection.
- New "Séries" page: each series is "On la regarde" (watching), "Plus tard" (later, with an optional
  reminder in 1, 3 or 6 months or on a date) or "Pas pour nous" (not for us), with its audience. Only
  watched series are scheduled. New Sonarr downloads land in "À classer" (to sort, administrator only).
  On first launch, series already started in Jellyfin are set to "On la regarde", the others to "Plus tard".
- "Composer la semaine" (Compose the week), from the Semaine page: the week's slots are empty cases and
  you place cards from a hand of five (next episodes, liked titles, suggested movies) by dragging them or
  by tapping the card, then the case. "Autre main" deals new cards, "Pas cette semaine" sets one aside,
  "C'est bon pour la semaine" confirms the week.
- After an update, browsers load the new interface straight away (no more stale page in Safari).

## Data

New files and fields in `/config`, ignored by v0.1.0 if you go back: `watchstate.json` (series states)
and, in `plans/<week>.json`, the fields `confirmed`, `picked`, `skipped_week` and `validated_at`. After an
update, the current week's proposals show as ideas until you confirm them.
